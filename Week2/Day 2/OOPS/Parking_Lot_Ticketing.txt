Pseudo Code: Parking Lot Ticketing System

Class Vehicle
    Attributes: vehicleNumber, entryTime
    Method: calculateFee(exitTime)

Class Car extends Vehicle
    Method: calculateFee(exitTime)
        Fee = 50 + (extra hours × 30)

Class Bike extends Vehicle
    Method: calculateFee(exitTime)
        Fee = 20 + (extra hours × 10)

Class Truck extends Vehicle
    Method: calculateFee(exitTime)
        Fee = 100 + (extra hours × 50)

Process:
    On entry → create subclass object with entry time
    On exit → call calculateFee(exitTime) and print details
