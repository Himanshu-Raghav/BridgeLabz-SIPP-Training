Pseudo Code: Attendance Marker

2D Array attendance[30][30]   // 30 students × 30 days

Operations:
    MarkAttendance(student, day, status)
    DailySummary(day)
        Count present students
    MonthlySummary(student)
        Count total presents for student
    DefaulterList()
        Find students < 75% attendance

Example:
    attendance[student][day] = 1 → Present
    attendance[student][day] = 0 → Absent
